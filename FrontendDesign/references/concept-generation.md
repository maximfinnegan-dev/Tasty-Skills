# Concept generation

A method for finding a visual direction that belongs to this product specifically,
for the moments the design read alone will not get you there: a new surface, a
brand expansion, or a full identity replacement. Read this between [stating the
design read](../SKILL.md#1-read-the-brief) and [setting the three
dials](../SKILL.md#2-set-the-three-dials) — it is how you arrive at a direction
worth setting dials for, not a replacement for either step.

Skip this for a local addition to an established surface, a narrow and precisely
specified request, or anything that inherits a direction that already exists. Full
concept generation is for the moment a direction is actually being chosen.

## Why the first idea is usually the rut

Asked for a visual direction, a first pass reliably lands on the category's own
default: the same two or three references every competitor in the space also
reaches for. That default is not wrong exactly — it is just not a choice, and
`ai-tells.md`'s litmus test exists because of how reliably this happens. The fix
is not trying harder on the first idea. It is generating enough real alternatives
that the first idea has to compete for its place rather than winning by default.

## Generate seven candidates, not one

1. **Name the mechanism and the audience's world in a sentence or two.** What does
   this product actually do that is specific to it, and who is the audience, in
   their own cultural and visual context — not a demographic label, but the
   graphic and screen traditions they actually live inside: the publications they
   read, the interfaces they use daily, the physical objects and rituals of their
   world.
2. **Name the category's two obvious answers first, out loud, and set them aside.**
   Every category has a page it always ships. Naming it is what keeps it from
   sneaking back in as candidate three dressed differently.
3. **List seven concrete visual systems, artifacts, or rituals from that world**
   that could plausibly carry the mechanism, each with one line on why it
   resonates. A named abstract system — a school of poster design, a notation
   standard, a documentation convention — counts as a concrete candidate; it does
   not have to be a physical object. Ask what this thing would look like as a
   physical object, or what its world looked like before it had a screen.
4. **Check the spread before going further.** If more than three of the seven
   share one material family (three variations on "brutalist concrete," say),
   the list stopped at the first idea that came easily. Dig until the seven span
   at least three genuinely different families. Near-duplicates count once.
5. **Turn the strongest two or three into complete directions** — each one a
   visual world paired with a concrete first-surface concept, not a mood board.
   This is where [design-direction.md](design-direction.md) and
   [ai-tells.md](ai-tells.md) take over: derive the actual token set and check
   the direction against the tells catalogue before building.

## The memory test

Before committing, describe what the first viewport of each candidate direction
would actually contain — not its atmosphere, its content. Then ask: if someone
saw only this and left, what would they describe an hour later?

If the honest answer is a mood or a color, the direction has not committed to
anything yet — it is decoration waiting for a concept. A direction that passes
the test names a mechanism, a specific piece of content, or a demonstrable
interaction, something a competitor could not paste into their own page and have
it make sense. The first viewport is the thesis; everything after it is support.

## When you are choosing between real alternatives

Weighing two or three directions that both survived the process above, judge on
exactly two axes: how clearly the audience recognizes themselves in it, and how
clearly the product's own facts come through. A direction that loses on both to
a stronger alternative should lose — that is the process working, not a failure
to commit. A direction that is merely different from the default without being
clearer on either axis is novelty, not a choice.
